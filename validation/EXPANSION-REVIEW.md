# Twelve-skill expansion: review and observed checks

Date: 31 October 2026. This update adds `se-proof`, `se-visualise`, and `se-swarm`; expands `se-who`; clarifies concurrent/verify boundaries; and updates both guides. The earlier nine-skill records remain historical. No installation or production validation is claimed.

## Responsibility and contradiction review

- Proof grounds claims and demonstrates causal understanding; verify executes or qualifies behavioral evidence and gates.
- Who chooses suitable expertise, access and accountability; swarm launches and adapts the team; concurrent governs ownership, isolation and integration.
- Visualise selects the representation for comprehension. Animation is appropriate when it reveals time, state or causality; static explanations remain available and motion does not establish truth.
- Existing authorization permits useful continued work and repairs. Genuine permission requirements, denied tools, human decision authority and resource limits are preserved. Iteration ends at evidenced acceptance or a concrete unresolved boundary, not arbitrary perfection.
- Independent source review found a README diagram route that bypassed pre-change trust review during delegated repair. The route was corrected through trust review and authorized implementation before integration. A narrow recheck found no remaining issue in that scope. No new gate vocabulary or mandatory sibling installation was introduced.
